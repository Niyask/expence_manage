Why Daily Expense?

Built for people who want a simple, attractive expense tracker without bank logins or subscriptions. Inspired by a clean iOS design system with teal and violet gradients, spring animations, and light or dark appearance.

🏠 Home dashboard Net balance, income vs expenses, and recent transactions.
➕ Quick add Log expenses or income with tags, notes, and dates.
📈 Financial report Today, this week, or this month with category breakdown.
🌙 Evening report End-of-day summary with category bars and savings view.
📋 All transactions Edit, delete, sort by date or amount; month → week grouping.
⚙️ Settings Currency, appearance, bedtime time, weekly insights toggle.

Data retention: The app keeps up to 2 months of transaction history on your device. Older entries are removed automatically to keep storage light.
