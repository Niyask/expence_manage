Getting started

  1. Install Daily Expense from the App Store (iOS 17+).
  2. Complete the onboarding screens (optional name for your greeting).
  3. Tap + to add an expense, or use Add Income on Home for salary and other credits.
  4. Open Settings to set currency, light/dark appearance, and bedtime reminder time.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data stored in the cloud?
No. Transactions and settings are saved only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
Will App Store updates delete my data?
Yes, when you update Daily Expense from the App Store over your existing install. Data is only lost if you delete the app from your iPhone (unless you restore from a backup).
How long is history kept?
Up to 2 months of transactions. Older entries are removed automatically.
Bedtime notifications are not appearing
  1. In the app: Settings → Bedtime Reminder — turn notifications on.
  2. Allow notifications when iOS prompts you.
  3. On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Daily Expense — ensure Allow Notifications is on.
  4. Test on a real device; the simulator may not behave like a phone.
How do I edit or delete a transaction?
Use All Transactions from Home, or long-press / tap a recent item on Home. Swipe or open the edit sheet to change amount, tag, note, or date.
What do weekly insights mean?
The app shows your top expense category for the selected week and compares spending to the previous week when the same category leads both weeks.
Export or backup to iCloud?
Export and cloud backup are planned; v1.0 stores data locally only. You can rely on an encrypted iPhone backup via iCloud or Finder.
Supported devices
iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later. iPad is not a primary target unless you enable universal support in Xcode.

Report a problem

When contacting support, please include:

  • iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • App version (Settings → Daily Expense at bottom of in-app Settings)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots if possible (no need to send financial amounts)
